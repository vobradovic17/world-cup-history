The profile

Lukas Podolski

Lukas Podolski was a German forward with a powerful left foot. He began his club career at Köln and became an important attacking option for Germany.

Podolski broke through at Köln and later played for Bayern Munich and Arsenal. His left-footed shooting and willingness to run directly at defenders made him a regular in Germany’s attack.

Podolski won the official Best Young Player award at the 2006 World Cup, where Germany finished third. He scored twice against Sweden in the round of 16.
