The profile

Emiliano Martínez

Emiliano Martínez is an Argentine goalkeeper who became a central figure in the national team after establishing himself in the English Premier League.

After years on Arsenal’s books and several loan spells, Martínez became a first-choice goalkeeper at Aston Villa. His confidence in one-on-one situations and penalty shootouts became a distinctive part of his game.

Martínez won the Golden Glove at the 2022 World Cup as Argentina became champion. His late save against France and his work in the final shootout were crucial to the victory.
