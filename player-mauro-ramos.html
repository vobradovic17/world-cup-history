The profile

Mauro Ramos

Mauro Ramos was a Brazilian central defender whose club career included Santos. He helped give Brazil’s back line stability alongside its celebrated attack.

Mauro played for São Paulo and Santos. As a central defender he offered disciplined marking and leadership behind a Brazil attack filled with creative players.

Mauro captained Brazil to the 1962 World Cup title in Chile. The team beat Czechoslovakia 3–1 in the final.
