The profile

Antonio Cabrini

Antonio Cabrini was an Italian left-back known for his ability to defend and join attacks. He spent much of his club career with Juventus.

Cabrini became a regular for Juventus and Italy. His ability to move up the left flank while maintaining defensive discipline gave both teams balance.

Cabrini emerged with Italy at the 1978 World Cup and is listed in retrospective young-player selections for that tournament. He later helped Italy win the 1982 World Cup.
