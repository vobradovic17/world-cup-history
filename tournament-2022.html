Qatar hosted the first World Cup in the Middle East in 2022, moving the competition to November and December. Argentina won its third title after a dramatic final against France, while Morocco became the first African and Arab semi-finalist.

Crowds from across the region filled the compact group of host cities. The winter schedule gave the edition a different rhythm, and Morocco's victories brought broad celebration among Arab and African supporters.

Saudi Arabia upset Argentina in the group stage, but Lionel Messi's side recovered. Japan beat Germany and Spain, and Morocco eliminated Spain on penalties before defeating Portugal to reach the semi-finals.

Argentina survived a tense quarter-final against the Netherlands on penalties and beat Croatia in the semi-final. France overcame Morocco to reach the title match. Messi scored twice there, while Kylian Mbappé's hat-trick repeatedly pulled France back into contention.

The final finished 3–3 after extra time before Argentina won the shootout 4–2. Messi completed his pursuit of a World Cup title, and Mbappé's eight goals earned the Golden Boot.
