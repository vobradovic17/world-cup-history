# Team imagery sources

- Association crest PNGs: [wc26-assets national team logos](https://github.com/Sachawd/wc26-assets/tree/main/national-team-logos). The Teams page uses the supplied crest where the archive has a matching association.
- Additional team badges: Wikimedia/Wikipedia files [Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Bgnatcrest.png), [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:China_national_football_team.svg), [Czechoslovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Czechoslovakian_FA_Logo.svg), [East Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:East_Germany_national_football_team_logo.svg), [El Salvador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:El_Salvador_crest.svg), [Hungary](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Coat_of_arms_of_Hungary.svg), [Kuwait](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kuwait_Football_Association_logo.png), [Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Romania_national_football_team_logo.svg), [Russia](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Coat_of_Arms_of_the_Russian_Federation_2.svg), and [Slovenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Slovenia_national_football_team.svg).
- Historical association badges: [Soviet Union](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Federatsiya_Futbola_SSSR_logo_(variant_2).svg) and [Yugoslavia](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Football_Association_of_Yugoslavia_logo_(1991-1992).svg).
- Angola, Bolivia, Israel, North Korea, Serbia and Montenegro, and Togo use their national flag as the team badge in their Wikipedia team infobox. The historical Dutch East Indies association is also represented by the Dutch flag in its [association entry](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/F%C3%A9d%C3%A9ration_des_Indes_orientales_n%C3%A9erlandaises_de_football). The page presents these flags in a badge frame alongside a small, rectangular flag.
- Northern Ireland uses the [Irish Football Association logo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Irish_Football_Association_logo.svg). Zaire uses its [historical national coat of arms](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Coat_of_arms_of_Zaire_(1971%E2%80%931997).svg); a football association crest from its 1974 appearance was not available in the inspected sources.
- Current national and home nation flags: [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons), MIT license, `flags/4x3` collection.
- Historical flags for East Germany, Serbia and Montenegro, and Zaire: Wikimedia Commons files [East Germany](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_East_Germany.svg), [Serbia and Montenegro](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Serbia_and_Montenegro.svg), and [Zaire](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Zaire_(1971%E2%80%931997).svg).
- Historical Soviet Union and Yugoslavia flags were already in this project's `assets` folder.
