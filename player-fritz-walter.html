The profile

Fritz Walter

Fritz Walter was an attacking midfielder whose club career was closely tied to Kaiserslautern. His influence on the pitch made him West Germany’s captain.

Walter spent his club career at Kaiserslautern, where he became a symbol of the team. His passing, organisation and understanding with teammates shaped West Germany’s attack.

Walter led West Germany to the 1954 World Cup title. The team came from behind to defeat Hungary 3–2 in the final in Bern.
