The profile

Carlos Bilardo

Carlos Bilardo was an Argentine coach who organised his teams around tactical discipline and flexibility. He became national-team manager before the 1986 World Cup.

Bilardo played and coached at Estudiantes before taking the Argentina job. As a manager he used carefully planned formations and asked players to adapt to different opponents.

Bilardo guided Argentina to the 1986 title, with Diego Maradona central to the campaign. Argentina beat West Germany 3–2 in the final and reached another final under him in 1990.
