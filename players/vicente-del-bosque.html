The profile

Vicente del Bosque

Vicente del Bosque was a Spanish coach who had previously managed Real Madrid. He gave Spain continuity and a settled midfield at international level.

Del Bosque managed Real Madrid to league and European success before becoming Spain coach. He retained the team’s passing foundation and helped it win the 2012 European Championship after the 2010 World Cup.

Del Bosque coached Spain to its first World Cup title in 2010. Spain beat the Netherlands 1–0 after extra time in the final.
