The profile

Michel Preud'homme

Michel Preud’homme was a Belgian goalkeeper known for reflex saves and positioning. He played for Standard Liège, Mechelen and Benfica.

Preud’homme represented Standard Liège, Mechelen and Benfica. His reflexes and command of the penalty area made him one of Belgium’s leading goalkeepers.

Preud’homme received the Yashin Award as the best goalkeeper of the 1994 World Cup. Belgium reached the round of 16 in the United States.
