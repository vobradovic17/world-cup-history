The profile

Alf Ramsey

Alf Ramsey played for England and later became a successful club manager before taking charge of the national team. He favoured a compact, disciplined side.

Ramsey played for Tottenham and England before becoming a manager. He led Ipswich Town to the English league title and then took charge of the national side.

Ramsey guided England to its only World Cup title in 1966. The hosts beat West Germany 4–2 after extra time in the final at Wembley.
