The profile

Enzo Scifo

Enzo Scifo was a creative Belgian midfielder who began his senior club career at Anderlecht. His passing and close control helped Belgium progress through major tournaments.

Scifo developed at Anderlecht and later played in Italy and France. He directed attacks with close control and passing while remaining a regular in Belgium’s midfield.

Scifo helped Belgium reach the 1986 World Cup semi-finals, its best finish at the time. He appears in retrospective selections of that tournament’s best young players.
