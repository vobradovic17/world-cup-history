The profile

Aymoré Moreira

Aymoré Moreira was a Brazilian coach who worked with the national team during its early World Cup successes. He took charge of the side for the 1962 tournament.

Moreira worked in Brazilian club and national-team football. He took over a squad that already had world champions and kept it competitive despite the loss of Pelé to injury during the tournament.

Moreira led Brazil to a second successive title in Chile in 1962. Brazil beat Czechoslovakia 3–1 in the final after progressing without the injured Pelé for much of the competition.
