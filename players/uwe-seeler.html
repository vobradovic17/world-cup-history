The profile

Uwe Seeler

Uwe Seeler was a West German forward who spent his club career at Hamburg. His movement and finishing made him a regular for the national team.

Seeler remained with Hamburg throughout his senior club career. He was a prolific centre-forward whose work rate and finishing helped him lead West Germany’s attack for many years.

Seeler made 21 World Cup appearances across four tournaments from 1958 to 1970. West Germany reached the 1966 final and finished third in 1970.
