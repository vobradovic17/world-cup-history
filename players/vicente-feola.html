The profile

Vicente Feola

Vicente Feola was a Brazilian coach who took the national team to the 1958 World Cup. His squad blended experienced players with emerging talents such as Pelé.

Feola coached São Paulo before leading Brazil. He brought together an experienced core and young attackers, giving Pelé and Garrincha room to influence matches.

Feola led Brazil to its first title in Sweden in 1958. Brazil beat the hosts 5–2 in the final.
