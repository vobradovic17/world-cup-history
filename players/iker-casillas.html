The profile

Iker Casillas

Iker Casillas was a goalkeeper for Real Madrid and Spain whose reflexes and leadership were central to both teams. He captained Spain during its most successful period.

Casillas came through Real Madrid’s academy and became the club’s goalkeeper while still a teenager. He won major domestic and European honours and provided Spain with a dependable last line of defence.

Casillas led Spain to the 2010 World Cup title and received the tournament’s Golden Glove. He kept a clean sheet in the 1–0 final win over the Netherlands.
