The profile

Gianpiero Combi

Gianpiero Combi was a goalkeeper who spent his club career with Juventus. He was a steady presence behind Italy’s defence in the early 1930s.

Combi played his entire club career at Juventus, winning multiple Italian league titles. His shot-stopping and authority made him an important part of Italy’s defence.

Combi captained Italy at the 1934 World Cup on home soil. Italy beat Czechoslovakia 2–1 after extra time in the final to claim its first title.
