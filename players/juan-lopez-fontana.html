The profile

Juan López Fontana

Juan López Fontana was a Uruguayan coach who led the national team during the 1950 World Cup in Brazil.

López worked with Uruguay’s national team during a period when the country remained a major force in South American football. He oversaw the squad through its 1950 campaign.

Under López, Uruguay won its second World Cup title. The team beat host Brazil 2–1 in the decisive final-round match at the Maracanã.
