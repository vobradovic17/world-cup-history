The profile

Mário Zagallo

Mário Zagallo was a Brazilian winger who became a coach after his playing career. His influence spans several generations of Brazilian football.

Zagallo played for Flamengo and Botafogo before becoming a coach. He gave his teams tactical balance, working hard as a winger and later organising talented attacking players from the touchline.

Zagallo scored in Brazil’s 1958 final victory over Sweden and coached Brazil to the 1970 title in Mexico, becoming the first person to win the World Cup as both player and coach. Brazil beat Italy 4–1 in the final.
