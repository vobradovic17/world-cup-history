The profile

Bellini

Hilderaldo Bellini was a central defender who played much of his club football for Vasco da Gama. He was a leader in the Brazil side of the late 1950s.

Bellini played for Vasco da Gama and São Paulo. His strength in central defence and his leadership helped Brazil’s younger attacking players play with confidence.

Bellini captained Brazil to its first World Cup title in 1958. The team defeated Sweden 5–2 in the final, and he lifted the trophy as Brazil’s captain.
