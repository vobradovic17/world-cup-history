The profile

Philipp Lahm

Philipp Lahm was a German full-back and midfielder whose club career was centred on Bayern Munich. His positioning and passing gave Germany flexibility.

Lahm spent most of his club career at Bayern Munich, apart from a loan at Stuttgart. He played on both sides of defence and in midfield, using positioning and passing to control games.

Lahm captained Germany to the 2014 World Cup title. The team beat Argentina 1–0 after extra time in the final in Rio de Janeiro.
