The profile

Marc Overmars

Marc Overmars was a Dutch winger known for acceleration and direct running. His club career included major spells with Ajax, Arsenal and Barcelona.

Overmars played for Ajax, Arsenal and Barcelona. His acceleration down the wing created chances for others and made him difficult to contain in open space.

Overmars helped the Netherlands reach the 1994 World Cup quarter-finals. He appears in retrospective selections of that edition’s best young players.
