The profile

Nicolás Otamendi

Nicolás Otamendi is an Argentine central defender whose club career has included Manchester City and Benfica. His tackling and positioning brought experience to the national side.

Otamendi played for Porto, Valencia and Manchester City before moving to Benfica. He combined physical defending with leadership as his club and international career continued into his late thirties.

Otamendi played 21 World Cup matches through 2026. He was part of Argentina’s 2022 title-winning defence and the team that reached the 2026 final.
