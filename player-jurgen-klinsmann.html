The profile

Jürgen Klinsmann

Jürgen Klinsmann was a German striker known for movement and finishing. His club career included spells in Germany, Italy, France and England.

Klinsmann played for Stuttgart, Inter, Tottenham and Bayern Munich among other clubs. His movement across the front line and sharp finishing made him a consistent scoring threat.

Klinsmann scored 11 World Cup goals across the 1990, 1994 and 1998 tournaments. He was part of the West Germany team that won the 1990 title.
