The profile

Luis de la Fuente

Luis de la Fuente developed players across Spain’s youth national teams before becoming senior-team coach. His side won the European Championship in 2024.

He coached Spain at youth level before taking the senior job in 2022. He led the team to the 2023 Nations League and UEFA EURO 2024 titles while bringing younger players into the squad.

De la Fuente led Spain to the 2026 World Cup title. Spain beat Argentina 1–0 after extra time in the final and conceded only once during the tournament.
