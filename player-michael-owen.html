The profile

Michael Owen

Michael Owen was an English forward whose pace and finishing brought him to prominence as a teenager at Liverpool.

Owen became a prolific Liverpool striker while still young and later played for Real Madrid, Newcastle and Manchester United. His acceleration helped him break behind defenders and finish quickly.

Owen scored a memorable solo goal against Argentina at the 1998 World Cup. He is listed in retrospective selections of that tournament’s best young players.
