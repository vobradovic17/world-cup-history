from the spot.

The United States hosted the 1994 World Cup, attracting enormous crowds as Brazil won its fourth title. Bulgaria and Sweden reached the semi-finals, while the final against Italy became the first to be decided by a penalty shootout.

Large American stadiums and summer heat gave the edition a distinctive setting. Supporters watched unexpected challengers progress alongside the traditional powers, and Brazil's Romário gave a disciplined team moments of attacking flair.

Oleg Salenko scored five in one match against Cameroon, a World Cup record. He and Bulgaria's Hristo Stoichkov finished level on six goals, even though Russia went out after the groups. Bulgaria then eliminated defending champion Germany on its way to the last four.

Brazil edged the Netherlands 3–2 in the quarter-finals, with Bebeto celebrating his goal by rocking his arms like a cradle. Roberto Baggio's decisive strikes carried Italy through the other half of the draw, setting up a final between two three-time champions in Pasadena.

Neither team scored in 120 minutes. Brazil won the shootout after Baggio's last kick flew over the bar, becoming the first four-time world champion and giving the tournament one of its most recognisable closing images.
