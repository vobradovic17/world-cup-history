The profile

Carlos Alberto Torres

Carlos Alberto Torres was an attacking right-back who played for Santos and became one of Brazil’s best-known captains. He brought width and composure to the side.

Carlos Alberto played for Santos and later the New York Cosmos. He attacked from right-back with well-timed runs while remaining a composed defender and leader.

He captained Brazil to the 1970 World Cup title and scored the final goal in its 4–1 victory over Italy. The move leading to his goal remains a celebrated example of the team’s passing play.
