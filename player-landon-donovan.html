The profile

Landon Donovan

Landon Donovan was an American attacking midfielder and forward who became one of the national team’s leading players. He combined pace with an eye for the final pass.

Donovan built his club career primarily in Major League Soccer, especially with San Jose and LA Galaxy. His pace, movement and final pass made him a central figure in the United States attack.

Donovan helped the United States reach the 2002 World Cup quarter-finals and appears in retrospective young-player selections for that edition. He later scored a stoppage-time winner against Algeria in 2010.
