The profile

Marcello Lippi

Marcello Lippi built a successful club coaching career in Italy, most notably with Juventus, before taking charge of the national team.

Lippi won domestic and European honours with Juventus before taking the Italy job. He was known for organising strong defences while giving creative players room to decide matches.

Lippi led Italy to the 2006 World Cup title. His side conceded only two goals during the tournament and beat France on penalties after a 1–1 final.
