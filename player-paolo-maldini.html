The profile

Paolo Maldini

Paolo Maldini was an Italian defender who spent his entire senior club career with AC Milan. He excelled at both left-back and centre-back.

Maldini became a long-serving AC Milan captain and won major domestic and European titles. He combined precise tackling with positional awareness, first at left-back and later at centre-back.

Maldini made 23 World Cup appearances across four tournaments from 1990 to 2002. Italy finished third in 1990 and reached the final in 1994 during that span.
